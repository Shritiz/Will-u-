For You

Two hearts met by chance,

Two souls chose to stay.

In every smile, a little sunrise,

In every glance, a brighter day.


May your laughter outlast the storms,

May your dreams always take flight.

Like two birds sharing the endless sky,

May you always find your way back to each other.


For love is not in grand gestures alone,

But in every little "I'm here" whispered through time.
